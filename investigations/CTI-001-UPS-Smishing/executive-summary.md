# Executive summary — CTI-001

On 24 November 2026, an unsolicited SMS in Romania impersonated UPS and requested resolution of alleged customs charges through a non-UPS domain. Passive checks reported that the domain had been registered approximately 16 hours 30 minutes earlier and received a suspicious reputation verdict.

**Assessment:** High confidence that the message warrants treatment as suspected smishing. No landing-page behavior, financial loss or attacker identity was verified.

**Business relevance:** Brand impersonation can expose customers to fraud and erode trust. Organizations should maintain clear reporting channels and monitor suspicious brand-related domains.

**Recommended action:** Verify charges independently via official carrier channels, preserve the message, and report suspected abuse. Do not visit the supplied link.
